## Data and geographic sources

School finance and household income values, district identifiers, and source boundaries are attributed to the **U.S. Census Bureau**. **NCES** documentation informed interpretation of school district geography and enrollment. The project transforms these public source files through selection, exact-identifier joining, formatting, and boundary simplification; the resulting visualization is an independent work. Neither Census nor NCES has endorsed the site or its interpretations.
